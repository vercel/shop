Shop Agent helps shoppers find products, choose options, ask store questions, and manage their cart through conversation.

It is **disabled by default**. The storefront works without it, and the chat control stays hidden until you enable it. Customer sign-in is not required.

## Enable Shop Agent

Shop Agent uses your storefront's existing Shopify connection. It runs on [Eve](https://eve.dev) with OpenAI GPT-5.6 Luna through [Vercel AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/ai-gateway).

1. Set `agent.isEnabled` to `true` in `lib/config/index.ts`.
2. Configure AI Gateway access. Vercel deployments can use deployment identity authentication. For local development or other hosts without that authentication, set `AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY` in your server environment. Use `.env.local` for local values and keep the key server-only. See [Environment Variables](/docs/reference/env-vars#shop-agent).
3. Run `pnpm dev`, open Shop Agent, and ask for a product from your store. The reply should show product cards you can open or ask about.

Before allowing public traffic, review [privacy and usage limits](#privacy-and-usage-limits). On Vercel, deploy the storefront with the setting and Gateway access configured; you do not need a separate agent deployment.

### Local production

For a local production build, run `pnpm build:agent && pnpm build`. Then run `pnpm exec eve start --port 4274` and `pnpm start` in separate terminals. Use `pnpm dev` for ordinary development.

## Shopper experience

Shoppers can:

- Search for products, narrow results by color or size, and browse collections.
- View product details, prices, availability, and variant choices.
- Ask for related or complementary products.
- Add items, change quantities, remove items, and add an order note.
- Ask about shipping, returns, payments, and other store policies.

Product and policy answers use Shopify data. Keep your catalog and store policies up to date so shoppers can get useful answers.

### One cart, with the shopper in control

Chat uses the same cart as the rest of the storefront. After a confirmed change, the assistant shows a short confirmation and opens the cart drawer. Shoppers complete their purchase through the storefront's checkout button; the assistant cannot place orders or process payments.

Checkout waits while the assistant is working or cart changes are being confirmed in that browser tab. If the cart cannot be refreshed, use **Refresh cart** in chat before continuing. This checks the cart without repeating the requested change. Wait for the response before checking out in another tab.

A cart change may still finish after a response is stopped or interrupted. A retry can also repeat a change. Check the cart before asking again, especially after a timeout or connection failure. See [Cart](/docs/anatomy/cart) for the rest of the cart experience.

### Continue a conversation or start over

The conversation and draft can be restored when shoppers navigate or reload in the same browser. Returning shoppers can reopen their conversation without starting over.

Use **Stop** to interrupt a response or **Clear chat** to start a new conversation. Clearing chat does not empty the cart.

Conversations have a one-day lifetime and a usage limit. If a conversation expires or reaches its limit, clear it to start a new one.

## Privacy and usage limits

Once enabled, chat is publicly accessible and messages can incur model charges. Add deployment-level rate limits and Gateway spending controls appropriate to your traffic. The per-conversation usage limit is not a store-wide spending cap.

Bot protection is also **disabled by default**. For Vercel deployments, you can enable `botid.isEnabled` in [Shop Configuration](/docs/reference/shop-config). Bot protection does not replace access controls or spending limits.

Customer sign-in does not make chat history private. The default setup does not restrict a conversation to its original shopper: anyone with its conversation ID may be able to read or control it. Do not share conversation IDs. Add authenticated conversation ownership before using chat for private account or support data.

Eve stores conversations on the server, and conversation content is sent to the model provider. Clearing chat or reaching the one-day expiry does not guarantee deletion of those records. Configure retention and access policies, and explain to shoppers how their messages are handled.

The assistant receives a summary of cart items rather than gift-card recipient details. Personal information shoppers type into chat still reaches the model provider. Avoid requesting sensitive information that is not needed for shopping.

## What’s next

Adapt the tone, product presentation, and recommendations to your store. Add store-specific guidance while keeping Shopify as the source of prices and availability. Choose models and usage limits that balance response quality, speed, and cost.
